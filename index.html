Research Interests

I am broadly interested in LLM-based agents that can reason, learn, and act reliably in open-ended environments. My current research focuses on two directions: reasoning for general-purpose agents, which studies how agents plan, learn from experience, and generalize across tasks and environments; and multi-agent systems, which studies how agents collaborate with, verify, and evaluate one another to solve problems beyond the reach of a single agent.
